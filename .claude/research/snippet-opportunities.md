# Featured Snippet Target Opportunities (Top 5)

> **Prepared by:** Agent 4 (Research & Intelligence)
> **Goal:** Steal "Position Zero" (the Featured Snippet block) from lower-quality competitors.

## 1. Target: "What is an AI Playbook?"
**Opportunity Size:** High
**Current Holder:** HubSpot (Generic, non-specific definition)
**Our Play:** 
We can win this with a structured definition. In our "What is Agentic AI" guide, we need a literal section:
`<h2>What is an AI Playbook?</h2>`
`<p>An AI playbook is a step-by-step, role-specific guide detailing how to use artificial intelligence tools to automate a specific workflow or achieve a measurable business outcome...</p>`

## 2. Target: "Best Free ChatGPT Alternative"
**Opportunity Size:** Massive
**Current Holder:** Zapier (List format snippet)
**Our Play:**
Google loves pulling numbered lists for "Best X" queries. We need a clean H2 (e.g. `## Top 5 Free ChatGPT Alternatives`), followed immediately by an `<ol>` or `<ul>` list where each list item is exactly the tool name. No fluff before the list. 

## 3. Target: "Claude vs ChatGPT Pricing"
**Opportunity Size:** High
**Current Holder:** Various Reddit threads (Unstructured)
**Our Play:**
Google pulls tables for pricing comparisons. We MUST ensure our ComparisonTable renders standard `<table>`, `<th>`, and `<td>` HTML elements (which it does). We just need to make sure the H2 immediately preceding the table is `## Claude vs ChatGPT Pricing Comparison`.

## 4. Target: "How to write an AI Prompt"
**Opportunity Size:** Very High
**Current Holder:** Coursera
**Our Play:**
We need an ordered list (`<ol>`) nested directly under `## 5 Steps to Writing the Perfect AI Prompt` in our guides. Each `<ol>` item should be bolded and short (e.g., `1. **Assign a Persona:** Tell the AI who it is.`).

## 5. Target: "Cursor AI IDE pricing"
**Opportunity Size:** Medium/High (Developer Niche)
**Current Holder:** Cursor's own site
**Our Play:**
While we won't outrank Cursor's literal pricing page, we CAN win the "Is Cursor Pro worth it?" snippet by having a bulleted list of "Pros of upgrading to Cursor Pro" in our tool review.
