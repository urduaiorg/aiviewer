## 1. Technical SEO & AEO Baseline: 9.5/10
Thanks to the foundational work by Agent 1 and Agent 3, AIViewer is currently punching significantly above its weight class technically. 

**Our Strengths (Where we crush the competition):**
* **Core Web Vitals:** Our Astro 5 static architecture is infinitely faster than the React SPA stacks used by Futurepedia and TAAFT. Google prioritizes speed, and we have the edge.
* **Schema Perfection:** Our JSON-LD structured data (`ReviewSchema`, `FAQSchema`, `BreadcrumbSchema`, `ArticleSchema`) is flawless. Most competitors neglect FAQ schema or butcher their Review schema.
* **AEO Readiness:** We are explicitly formatting content for Perplexity and SearchGPT using the 2-sentence `AnswerBox` and question-led H2s. 
* **Content Freshness:** While Wirecutter takes 6 months to update a guide, we are indexed with March 2026 realities (e.g., GPT-5.4, Claude Opus 4.6). Answer engines heavily weight recency.

## 2. The Current Threat Landscape & Our Positioning
We are caught between two distinct types of competitors, and our strategy must exploit the weaknesses of both.

### Threat A: The "Mass Directory" Aggregators (There's An AI For That, Futurepedia)
* **Their Moat:** Massive volume (tens of thousands of scraped tools) and high legacy domain authority.
* **Their Weakness:** Zero editorial depth, high bounce rates, UI clutter, and pay-to-play bias. Google's Helpful Content updates are increasingly punishing this exact model.
* **Our Counter-Strategy:** Engage users. Our "Wirecutter" approach (fewer tools, 10x deeper reviews, `ProsCons`, `ComparisonTable`) will yield vastly superior "Time on Page" and lower bounce rates. We don't need to out-index them; we need to out-engage them on the top 100 highest-intent tool searches.

### Threat B: The "Editorial Heavyweights" (Wirecutter, TechRadar, Zapier Blog)
* **Their Moat:** Pristine E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness) and massive backlink profiles. Google blindly trusts them.
* **Their Weakness:** They are slow, generalized publishers. Their AI content is often surface-level and outdated by the time it publishes.
* **Our Counter-Strategy:** Niche supremacy and extreme freshness. By the time they review Claude 3.5, we are publishing workflows for Claude Opus 4.6.

## 3. The Critical Gap: Domain Authority & Backlinks
**Our Current Rating: 3/10**

We have the best technical foundation and the best on-page content, but **we are missing the off-page authority signals (backlinks)** required to rank #1 against legacy domains.

Other websites, journalists, and universities do not link to "Tool Directories." They link to:
1. Original Data & Statistics
2. Journalistic Reports on Industry Trends
3. Comprehensive Regulation Trackers

### The Solution: The "AI & Society" E-E-A-T Engine
To bridge our Domain Authority gap, we must immediately deploy the **AI & Society / Reports** vertical. These are not bottom-funnel "buyer" pages; these are top-funnel "link-bait" and authority pages.

**High-Priority Targets:**
* **"Global AI Regulation Tracker 2026":** Journalists covering tech law will link to our tracker if it is the most updated on the internet.
* **"The State of AI in Education 2026":** Academic institutions and education bloggers will cite our report.
* **"AI & Labor: Which Jobs are Evolving?":** The mainstream press will cite these insights.
