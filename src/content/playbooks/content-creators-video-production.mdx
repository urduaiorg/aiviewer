## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can AI completely edit a YouTube video?
Not yet. AI can perform raw cuts, remove silences, and add captions automatically, but narrative pacing and creative effects still require a human editor.

### Are AI-generated voices monetizable on YouTube?
Yes, YouTube generally allows monetization of high-quality AI voiceovers, provided the content itself is original and not repetitive or spammy.

### What is the best AI tool for generating B-roll?
Runway Gen-4.5 Alpha and OpenAI Sora are currently the leading tools for generating cinematic, hyper-realistic B-roll footage.

### Does AI save money on video production?
Yes, by replacing stock footage subscriptions, automated captioning services, and basic editing time, creators can significantly reduce overhead.
