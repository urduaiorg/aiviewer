## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is using AI for research considered plagiarism?
Using AI to find sources or outline a paper is generally acceptable research assistance, but submitting AI-generated text as your own writing is considered academic misconduct.

### Can AI write my research paper for me?
While AI can generate text, doing so violates academic integrity policies at almost all institutions and deprives you of learning the material.

### What is the best AI for academic research?
Perplexity AI is widely considered the best because it acts as an answer engine that pulls from and cites live web sources, including academic databases.

### How do I cite an AI in my bibliography?
Most major style guides (APA, MLA, Chicago) now have specific formats for citing generative AI. You should consult your professor's syllabus for their preferred method.
