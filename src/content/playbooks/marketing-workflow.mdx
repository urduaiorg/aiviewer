## 2. The Golden Framework: Context, Intent, Formula

A terrible prompt: *"Write a Facebook ad for my new accounting software."*

**A 2026 prompt follows the C-I-F formula:**
1. **Context:** Who are you and who is the audience?
2. **Intent:** What is the primary goal of this copy?
3. **Formula:** What exact copywriting framework should the AI use?

## 3. SEO Content: Beating the "AI Penalty"

Google's algorithms in 2026 easily detect "thin," generic AI content. To rank on Page 1, you cannot just tell the AI to write an article. You must provide the *human* insights for the AI to format.

### The "SME Interview" Prompt
**Step 1:** Record a 5-minute raw audio braindump of your personal thoughts on a topic (e.g., "Why link building is dead"). Use an AI transcriber like Otter.ai.

**Step 2:** Paste the unformatted, messy transcript into Claude or ChatGPT with the following prompt:

<PromptCard 
  title="The Subject Matter Expert (SME) Ghostwriter"
  prompt="Here is a raw transcript of my thoughts on the current state of SEO. Turn this into a 1,200-word blog post optimized for the keyword 'Link Building 2026'. Do NOT add any outside opinions or generic filler. Only use the arguments I made in the transcript. Format it with H2 question headers, short punchy paragraphs, and a contrarian tone."
  useCase="Writing high-ranking SEO content that sounds uniquely human."
  proTip="By restricting the AI to ONLY use the arguments from your transcript, you bypass the generic 'AI voice' entirely. You provide the soul; the AI provides the grammar and formatting."
/>

## 4. The "Roast My Landing Page" Strategy

AI is not just for writing; it is excellent for critiquing. 

If your landing page is failing to convert, you can feed the copy into ChatGPT and ask it to adopt the persona of your most cynical customer.

### The "Conversion Killer" Prompt
<PromptCard 
  title="The Cynic"
  prompt="Here is the complete copy for my new product landing page. Adopt the persona of a highly skeptical, budget-conscious customer who has been burned by similar products before. Tear this copy apart. Tell me exactly why you wouldn't buy this, which claims sound fake or unsubstantiated, and where you got bored and stopped reading."
  useCase="Identifying friction points in a sales funnel before you spend ad money."
  proTip="Do this before you run any paid traffic to a new page. The AI will often spot glaring holes in your value proposition that you were too close to the project to see."
/>
