## How Do You Automate Frontline Support?

Once you are comfortable with AI drafting, consider integrating an AI chatbot into your website to handle initial queries, passing only the complex issues to your human team.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will an AI chatbot annoy my customers?
Poorly configured chatbots annoy customers. Modern AI bots powered by custom knowledge bases provide instant, accurate answers and should always offer a clear path to a human agent.

### Do I need a developer to set up an AI support bot?
No, many platforms now offer "no-code" interfaces where you simply upload your website URL and FAQ documents to train the AI instantly.

### How much does AI customer service software cost?
Entry-level AI inbox assistants can cost $20-$50/month, while advanced autonomous frontline bots typically range from $100 to $500/month depending on volume.

### Can AI handle refunds and returns?
Yes, if integrated with your e-commerce platform (like Shopify or Stripe) via API, an AI agent can securely process standard returns and refunds based on your policy rules.
