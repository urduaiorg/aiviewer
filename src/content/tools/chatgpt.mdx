## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is ChatGPT free to use?
Yes, ChatGPT offers a free tier that gives you unlimited access to GPT-5.3 Instant and limited access to GPT-5.4. However, during peak traffic times, free users may face stricter usage limits on GPT-5.4 and will not have access to GPT-5.4 Thinking.

### Does ChatGPT save my data?
By default, OpenAI may use your conversations to train their models. You can turn this off in your Data Controls settings by toggling off "Chat History & Training."

### What is the difference between ChatGPT and ChatGPT Plus?
ChatGPT Plus costs $20/month and gives you extended limits on GPT-5.4, full access to GPT-5.4 Thinking for deep web research, native computer-use capabilities, DALL-E image generation, and the ability to build Custom GPTs.

### Can ChatGPT browse the live internet?
Yes, ChatGPT has a built-in "Browse with Bing" feature that allows it to search the web for up-to-date information, though it is sometimes slower than dedicated search engines like [Perplexity](/tools/perplexity-ai/).
