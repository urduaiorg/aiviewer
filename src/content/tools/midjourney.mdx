Midjourney is an industry-leading AI image generator renowned for its stunning photorealism and artistic stylization. With the release of version 8 in March 2026, it allows creative professionals to turn natural language prompts into high-fidelity concept art, marketing assets, and illustrations directly via a powerful web app.

## Key Features

- **Incredible Realism:** Version 8 significantly improved photorealism, moving to a new architecture for native 2K+ resolution.
- **Stylization:** It has a natural bias toward making things look "good" or cinematic, even with simple prompts, enhanced by the new "oref" styling system.
- **Text Rendering:** Version 8 can reliably generate accurate text within images (like a neon sign or a logo on a shirt).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Midjourney free to use?
No, Midjourney no longer offers a free trial tier. You must purchase a subscription, starting at $10/month, to generate any images.

### Can I use Midjourney images commercially?
Yes, if you are a paying subscriber, you own the assets you create and can use them commercially. However, courts have currently ruled that AI-generated images cannot be copyrighted in the US.

### How do I use Midjourney without Discord?
Midjourney now offers a fully redesigned web app interface, removing the previous requirement to use Discord. You can generate, manage, and scale your images directly in your browser.

### Is Midjourney better than DALL-E 3?
Midjourney is generally considered better for artistic, highly stylized, and photorealistic images. DALL-E 3 (inside ChatGPT) is better at following strict prompt instructions and generating text.
