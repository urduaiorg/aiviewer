Midjourney remains the undisputed king of AI image generation when it comes to sheer aesthetic quality. While DALL-E 3 may be easier to prompt, Midjourney produces art that often looks indistinguishable from professional photography or high-end digital illustration.

## Key Features

- **Incredible Realism:** Version 6 significantly improved photorealism, skin textures, and lighting.
- **Stylization:** It has a natural bias toward making things look "good" or cinematic, even with simple prompts.
- **Text Rendering:** Unlike earlier versions, V6 can reliably generate short, accurate text within images (like a neon sign or a logo on a shirt).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Midjourney free to use?
No, Midjourney no longer offers a free trial tier. You must purchase a subscription, starting at $10/month, to generate any images.

### Can I use Midjourney images commercially?
Yes, if you are a paying subscriber, you own the assets you create and can use them commercially. However, courts have currently ruled that AI-generated images cannot be copyrighted in the US.

### How do I use Midjourney without Discord?
Midjourney now offers a web interface (alpha.midjourney.com) for users who have generated over 100 images. New users still must start via the Discord interface.

### Is Midjourney better than DALL-E 3?
Midjourney is generally considered better for artistic, highly stylized, and photorealistic images. DALL-E 3 (inside ChatGPT) is better at following strict prompt instructions and generating text.
