Notion is already a powerhouse for knowledge management and project tracking. Notion AI takes that existing workspace and superpowers it by bringing an LLM directly to where your data lives.

## Key Features

- **Q&A Search:** Instead of searching for a document, you can ask Notion AI a question, and it will search your entire workspace and synthesize an answer with citations.
- **Database Autofill:** Create a column in a Notion database and ask the AI to automatically populate it based on the contents of the page (e.g., automatically generating summaries for 100 meeting notes).
- **Inline Editing:** Highlight text to instantly translate it, make it longer/shorter, or change the tone.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Notion AI included in the free plan?
Notion AI is not fully included. You get a very limited number of complimentary AI responses to test it out, after which it costs $10/month per user as an add-on to any Notion plan.

### Can Notion AI read my private pages?
Notion AI respects your existing workspace permissions. It will only search and reference pages that you personally have access to read. It cannot access pages you are restricted from.

### What is the difference between Notion AI and ChatGPT?
ChatGPT is a standalone chatbot that knows general internet knowledge. Notion AI is integrated directly into your workspace; its primary value is its ability to read, summarize, and query your own company's specific documents.

### Can I turn Notion AI off?
Yes, workspace owners can disable Notion AI for their entire workspace if they have privacy or compliance concerns.
