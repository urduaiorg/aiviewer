## What You'll Learn

IBM SkillsBuild offers two flagship AI learning paths:

**AI Fundamentals (10–20 hours)**
- What AI is and how it works
- Machine learning concepts without code
- Responsible and ethical AI
- AI in business and society

**Generative AI for Beginners (6–10 hours)**
- How large language models work
- Prompt engineering basics
- Real-world generative AI use cases
- Hands-on exercises with IBM tools

Both courses are free and earn an **IBM-verified digital credential** (Open Badge) shareable directly to LinkedIn.

## Who It's For

- Anyone who wants a verifiable IBM credential at no cost
