## What It Funds

A $30 million global initiative supporting organizations that use AI to transform public services. Projects must address one of three priority areas:

- **Health** — Improving healthcare access and frontline worker capacity
- **Resilience** — Enabling crisis forecasting and community recovery planning
- **Economy** — Enhancing infrastructure efficiency and affordability

## Who Can Apply

Nonprofits, social enterprises, and academic institutions that partner with government agencies. Projects must demonstrate:

- **Impact** — Data-driven solutions with measurable outcomes
- **Innovation** — AI applications beyond incremental improvements
- **Feasibility** — Realistic execution plans with documented government partnerships
- **Scalability** — Potential for replication across regions and use cases

## Details

- **Amount:** $1M to $3M USD per selected organization
- **Total fund:** $30 million
- **Deadline:** April 3, 2026
- **Additional support:** Multi-month Google Accelerator programme with AI strategy curriculum, responsible governance training, and Google Cloud credits
- **Pro bono support:** Technical assistance from Google teams included
