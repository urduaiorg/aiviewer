## What It Is

The AWS AI & ML Scholars Program is a free global scholarship co-developed by Amazon Web Services and Udacity, designed to bring foundational AI and machine learning skills to aspiring tech professionals worldwide — including those with no prior experience. AWS commits to sponsoring up to 100,000 learners in 2026.

## Two-Phase Structure

**Phase 1 — Challenge (March 24 – June 24, 2026)**
Learners access free AI & ML training content on Udacity's platform. All learners who complete the Challenge phase receive a 3-month AWS Skill Builder subscription at no cost.

**Phase 2 — Nanodegree (Invitation Only)**
The top 4,500 performers advance to a fully-funded, 3-month Udacity Nanodegree program — one of three tracks:
- AI Programmer
- Agentic AI Business Professional
- Agent Developer

## What You'll Learn

- Foundations of artificial intelligence and machine learning
- Hands-on practice with AWS tools including PartyRock, Amazon Q, and Amazon Bedrock
- How to prepare for the **AWS Certified AI Practitioner** certification
- Portfolio-building projects and career development pathways

## Who It's For

Anyone 18 or older who wants to build AI skills — no prior AI, coding, or cloud experience required. The program is open globally (where permitted).
