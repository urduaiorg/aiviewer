## What It Is

Elements of AI is a free online course series created by the University of Helsinki and MinnaLearn — the most popular course in the University of Helsinki's history, with over 2 million learners enrolled across 170+ countries. It was originally built to demystify AI for everyday people and has grown into a globally recognized program.

There are two courses in the series:

**Part 1 — Introduction to AI**
A non-technical introduction for anyone curious about what AI is, how it works, and how it shapes daily life. No math or programming required.

**Part 2 — Building AI**
A more hands-on course that introduces the algorithms behind AI. Requires basic Python programming knowledge.

## What You'll Learn

- What AI is (and isn't) — clearing up myths and misconceptions
- Real-world AI applications and limitations
- How machine learning works at a conceptual level
- The societal impact of AI on jobs, privacy, and decision-making
- Basic Python for AI (Part 2 only)

## Who It's For

Designed specifically for non-technical people — teachers, business professionals, policymakers, parents, and anyone who wants to understand AI without needing a technical background. Part 1 requires zero prerequisites.
