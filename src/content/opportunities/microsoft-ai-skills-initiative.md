## What You'll Learn

Microsoft's AI Skills Initiative offers structured learning paths covering:

- AI fundamentals and core concepts
- Working with Microsoft Copilot across Office apps
- Introduction to Azure AI services
- Responsible AI principles and practices
- Generative AI for productivity

## Who It's For

Professionals looking to integrate AI into their Microsoft-powered workflows. Suitable for beginners with separate tracks for more experienced users who want to build with Azure AI.

## Details

- **Duration:** Multiple paths, 2-20 hours each
- **Cost:** Free
- **Certificate:** LinkedIn-compatible badges available
- **Prerequisites:** None for beginner paths
