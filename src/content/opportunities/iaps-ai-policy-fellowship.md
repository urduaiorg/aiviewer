## What It Is

The IAPS AI Policy Fellowship is a fully funded 3-month professional fellowship for people who want to work on AI governance and policy. The program is run by the Institute for AI Policy and Strategy (IAPS), a nonpartisan research organization focused on the governance of advanced AI systems.

**What Fellows Do**
- Conduct research on AI governance, safety standards, and regulatory frameworks
- Engage with policymakers, researchers, and technical AI experts
- Produce policy memos, reports, or analysis for public or government audiences
- Build professional networks in the AI policy ecosystem

## Who It's For

- Early-career professionals transitioning into AI policy
- Researchers and analysts with background in technology, law, public policy, or social science
- Practitioners from government, nonprofits, academia, or industry who want to focus on AI governance
- People interested in AI safety, international AI governance, or domestic regulatory frameworks

## About IAPS

The Institute for AI Policy and Strategy is a research organization focused on ensuring advanced AI is developed and governed safely. IAPS publishes policy research and engages directly with government and industry stakeholders. Their fellowship is one of the few structured pathways into AI policy work for professionals who don't come from a pure computer science background.
