## What It Is

IBM SkillsBuild offers a free, structured AI learning path for adult learners — covering everything from AI fundamentals to machine learning and generative AI. No subscription or payment required. After completing the "Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals" learning plan, learners earn a verified digital credential (badge) directly from IBM.

The platform hosts 1,000+ free courses across technology disciplines, with the AI track being one of its most popular.

**Beginner:**
- Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (1h 15m)
- Introduction to Generative AI (1h 30m)

**Intermediate:**

**Advanced:**

## Who It's For

Professionals and learners at any level who want credentialed, structured AI training from a globally recognized technology company — without paying for a course or subscription. Particularly useful for job seekers looking to demonstrate AI literacy to employers.
