## What You'll Learn

The AWS AI & ML Scholars Program runs in two phases:

**Phase 1 — Challenge Phase (Free, 100,000 seats)**
- Prep for the AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam
- 3-month AWS Skill Builder subscription (value: $99)
- Access to AWS cloud labs and practice exams

**Phase 2 — Nanodegree (Top 4,500 earn full funding)**
Choose one of three tracks:
- **AI Programmer** — building AI-powered applications
- **Agentic AI Business Professional** — deploying AI in organizational workflows
- **Agent Developer** — engineering autonomous AI systems

## Who It's For

- Anyone 18+ globally — no prior experience required
- Professionals looking to pivot into AI/ML roles
- Students seeking industry-recognized credentials
- In 2025, 50,000+ learners from 170+ countries participated

## How It Works

1. Enroll in the Challenge Phase at the link above
2. Complete the AI Practitioner learning modules within the deadline
3. Top-scoring participants are selected for a fully funded Udacity Nanodegree
4. Nanodegree tracks include career support and portfolio projects

This is one of the most accessible pathways to an AWS certification that exists — 100,000 spots means most applicants who start can complete Phase 1.
