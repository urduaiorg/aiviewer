## What It Is

The AWS Imagine Grant is Amazon's flagship funding program for nonprofits, providing cash grants, AWS cloud credits, and technical implementation support to organizations using technology — especially AI — to advance their mission. Since 2018, AWS has awarded over $21 million in grants to nonprofits across three continents.

The 2026 application cycle is currently open.

## Three Award Categories

### Pathfinder Award
For groundbreaking, mission-critical projects at the frontier of artificial intelligence.
- Up to **$200,000 USD** financial grant
- Up to **$100,000 USD** in AWS credits
- Implementation support from the AWS Generative AI Innovation Center

### Go Further, Faster Award
For highly innovative projects that leverage cloud technology.
- Up to **$150,000 USD** financial grant
- Up to **$100,000 USD** in AWS credits
- AWS technical specialist guidance

### Momentum to Modernize Award
For foundational technology transformation and modernization.
- Up to **$50,000 USD** financial grant
- Up to **$20,000 USD** in AWS credits
- AWS technical specialist support

## What You'll Get

- Direct financial grant (cash)
- AWS cloud service credits
- Technical guidance from AWS specialists
- Access to the AWS Generative AI Innovation Center (Pathfinder track)
- Visibility among AWS's nonprofit partner network

## Who It's For

- 501(c) nonprofits based in the United States
- Regional variants available for Australia, New Zealand, Canada, and the UK
