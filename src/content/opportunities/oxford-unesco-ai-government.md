## What You'll Learn

A programme created by the University of Oxford Said Business School and UNESCO for public sector professionals. Covers:

- Digital transformation strategy for government services
- AI fundamentals and applications in the public sector
- Data governance and ethical AI frameworks
- Practical approaches to digital service delivery

## Who It's For

Public sector employees at any level of government — local, national, or regional:

- Public administrators and policymakers
- Digital or data transformation project managers
- Service delivery and innovation leaders
- ICT, legal, and compliance professionals in government
- Civil servants interested in AI literacy and data governance

No technical background required.

## Details

- **Cost:** Completely free (fully funded by UNESCO)
- **Duration:** Approximately 12 guided hours plus optional supplementary materials
- **Format:** Online, self-paced — complete on your own schedule
- **Certificate:** Official University of Oxford Certificate of Completion from Said Business School, in partnership with UNESCO (digital format)
- **Languages:** English and Spanish (French and Portuguese coming soon)
- **Prerequisites:** None — designed for non-technical professionals
