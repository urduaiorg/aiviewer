## What It Is

The Mozilla Fellows Program supports up to 10 leaders annually who are working at the intersection of technology and society. Fellows work on issues including:

- Trustworthy AI and algorithmic accountability
- AI's impact on journalism and media
- Internet privacy and surveillance
- Digital inclusion and access
- AI governance and policy

**Two tracks:**
- **Embedded Fellows** — placed within Mozilla partner civil society organizations
- **Independent Fellows** — pursue self-directed projects with Mozilla support

## Who It's For

- Researchers, journalists, technologists, and advocates working on AI's societal impact
- Leaders with demonstrated track records in tech policy, digital rights, or responsible AI
- Eligible regions: Kenya, South Africa, Brazil, MENA, Mexico, UK, US, EU

This is a competitive fellowship — Mozilla seeks fellows with existing expertise and a specific project vision, not entry-level applicants.

## Mozilla Foundation Context

Mozilla Foundation (the nonprofit behind Firefox) has been funding internet health and AI accountability work since 2017. Past fellows have gone on to lead policy at major institutions, publish research cited in legislation, and found advocacy organizations. The fellowship carries significant credibility in the tech policy world.
