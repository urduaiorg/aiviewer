## What It Is

AI4ALL Ignite is a 20-week virtual career accelerator for undergraduate students in technical majors. The program is fully free and designed to help students from underrepresented backgrounds break into AI careers through:

- **Hands-on AI portfolio projects** — build real projects you can show employers
- **Industry mentorship** — matched with professionals working in AI
- **Career readiness training** — resume reviews, interview prep, networking
- **Community** — cohort of peers with shared goals
- **Certificate of completion** — awarded at program end

## Who It's For

- U.S. undergraduate students in Computer Science, Data Science, or related technical majors
- Students with at least basic Python and foundational math (linear algebra, statistics)
- Students from underrepresented groups in tech are especially encouraged to apply
- Summer 2026 cohort commitment: ~85–90 hours over 20 weeks

## About AI4ALL

AI4ALL is a nonprofit working to increase diversity and inclusion in AI. The Ignite accelerator has placed alumni at companies including Google, Amazon, Microsoft, and AI research labs. It's one of the few free, structured AI career programs designed specifically to support students who lack access to industry networks.

**Apply before April 13, 2026** — this is a hard deadline for the Summer 2026 cohort.
