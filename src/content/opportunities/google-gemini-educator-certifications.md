## What's Available

Google for Education offers multiple certification tracks, including free Gemini AI certifications:

### Free Gemini Certifications
- **Gemini Certified Educator** — For K-12 teachers demonstrating proficiency with Gemini AI in the classroom
- **Gemini Certified Student (13+)** — For students aged 13 and up
- **Gemini Certified Faculty** — For higher education instructors
- **Gemini Certified Student (18+)** — For university-level students

### Paid Google Educator Certifications
- **Google Certified Educator Level 1** ($10) — Foundational Google Workspace mastery
- **Google Certified Educator Level 2** ($25) — Advanced technology integration skills

## Who It's For

- K-12 teachers integrating AI into their classrooms
- University professors and faculty
- Students wanting a recognized AI credential
- Education administrators exploring AI tools

## Details

- **Cost:** Gemini certifications are completely free; Educator Level 1/2 have small fees ($10/$25, free for select languages)
- **Validity:** All certifications valid for 3 years
- **Format:** Complete training modules, then pass the assessment exam
- **Badge:** Digital credential you can add to your resume, portfolio, or website
- **Prerequisites:** Complete the relevant Google training modules before taking the exam
