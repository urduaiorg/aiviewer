## What It Is

The Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation's Philanthropic Partnerships Team launched this Grand Challenge to fund organizations using artificial intelligence to transform how charitable giving works. The core question: **How might AI help donors give more, and give sooner?**

The challenge opened March 17, 2026, with a deadline of April 28, 2026. Successful applicants receive up to **$150,000 USD** for a project lasting up to one year.

## Three Focus Areas

1. **Connecting donors with causes** — tools that help donors discover and identify giving opportunities aligned with their values
2. **Converting intent into donations** — reducing friction between deciding to give and actually giving
3. **Building philanthropic data infrastructure** — foundational AI-ready data systems that improve the quality of philanthropic decision-making

## What You'll Get

- Up to **$150,000 USD** in funding
- Up to **1 year** of grant support
- Participation in up to three peer learning convenings with other grantees
- Direct engagement with the Gates Foundation's Philanthropic Partnerships Team

## Who It's For

- Nonprofit and civil society organizations
- Mission-driven for-profit entities (where AI is used for public benefit, not core commercial development)
- Consortia and collaborative partnerships (one lead organization required)

**Ineligible:** Individual applicants, government agencies, and academic institutions applying independently.
