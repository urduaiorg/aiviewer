## What You'll Learn

NVIDIA's Deep Learning Institute (DLI) offers hundreds of hands-on, GPU-accelerated courses across:

- **Generative AI** — large language models, diffusion models, RAG pipelines
- **Accelerated Computing** — CUDA programming, GPU optimization
- **Data Science** — end-to-end ML workflows with RAPIDS
- **Robotics & Edge AI** — deploying models on embedded devices
- **Computer Vision** — image classification, object detection, semantic segmentation

All courses run entirely in the cloud — no GPU hardware required.

## Who It's For

- Developers, data scientists, and researchers at any level
- Engineers looking to gain hands-on GPU computing skills
- Anyone seeking a verifiable credential from a hardware leader in AI

## How to Access Free Courses

On NVIDIA's training catalog, filter by "Free" to surface no-cost options. Free courses include introductions to deep learning, transformer networks, and generative AI fundamentals. Certificates are issued for all completed courses regardless of cost.
