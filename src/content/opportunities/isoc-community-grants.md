## What It Covers

The Internet Society offers multiple grant programs for community-driven internet projects:

- Expanding internet access in underserved areas
- Building digital literacy and skills programs
- Strengthening community networks
- Supporting local internet governance initiatives

## Who Can Apply

Nonprofits, community organizations, academic institutions, and Internet Society chapters working on internet-related projects. Projects must demonstrate clear community impact and sustainability.

## Details

- **Amount:** $5,000 - $30,000 (varies by program)
- **Cycle:** Rolling applications with periodic deadlines
- **Duration:** Typically 6-12 month project periods
- **Regions:** Global, with focus on developing regions
