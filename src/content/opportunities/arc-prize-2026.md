## What It Is

ARC Prize is an open competition challenging participants to solve the ARC-AGI benchmarks — a set of reasoning tasks designed to test general intelligence rather than pattern memorization. The 2026 competition has a **$2 million total prize pool** across multiple tracks.

**Prize Structure**
- **ARC-AGI-2 Grand Prize:** $425,000 (requires 85%+ accuracy + open-source release)
- **ARC-AGI-3 Milestone Prize 1:** $25,000 (deadline: June 30, 2026)
- **ARC-AGI-3 Milestone Prize 2:** $10,000 (deadline: September 30, 2026)
- **ARC-AGI-3 Milestone Prize 3:** $2,500 (deadline: September 30, 2026)
- Additional prizes for runner-up submissions

The competition is hosted on Kaggle. All submissions are evaluated against hidden test sets.

## Who It's For

- AI researchers and engineers working on reasoning, abstraction, and generalization
- Students and independent researchers who want a serious technical challenge
- Teams or individuals — no institutional affiliation required
- Anyone willing to work with Kaggle's platform and submit code solutions

## About ARC-AGI

The ARC-AGI benchmark was created by François Chollet (creator of Keras) to measure general reasoning — the kind of flexible problem-solving that current AI systems struggle with. ARC Prize is backed by a $1M+ philanthropic commitment and is one of the most scientifically significant AI competitions currently active. Winning or placing highly is a meaningful research credential.
