## What It Is

NVIDIA Inception is a free program that equips AI startups with technical resources, training, and network access. It is not a funding program — there's no equity taken and no cash grant — but the in-kind value can be substantial:

**What Members Get**
- **Free DLI training credits** — access to NVIDIA's Deep Learning Institute courses
- **Cloud GPU credits** — eligible startups can receive up to $150,000 via NVIDIA partner programs (e.g., Nebius AI Lift)
- **Early SDK access** — pre-release access to NVIDIA toolkits and frameworks
- **Preferred hardware pricing** — discounts on NVIDIA hardware through authorized resellers
- **VC & partner network** — introductions to investors and enterprise customers
- **Co-marketing opportunities** — feature placement in NVIDIA's startup ecosystem

## Who It's For

- Incorporated AI startups (must have a working website)
- Companies under 10 years old working with AI or ML in their product
- Startups at any stage — pre-seed through Series B+
- 19,000+ companies from 90+ countries are current members (as of March 2026)

## Details

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | Free to join — no fees, no equity |
| **Deadline** | None — rolling applications, always open |
| **Eligibility** | Incorporated company, under 10 years, working AI/ML product |
| **Application** | ~10-minute online application at the URL above |

## How to Apply

Visit the Inception page and click "Apply Now." You'll answer questions about your company's AI use case, stage, and technical stack. Most applications receive a decision within 1–2 weeks. Benefits unlock progressively as your relationship with NVIDIA deepens.
