**Where AI Ustad fits**

PakEducate describes AI Ustad as its built-in assistant for school staff. Documented uses include drafting Urdu and English notices, preparing result comments, explaining reports and guiding users to relevant modules. The provider explicitly says staff should review AI output because it can be wrong or incomplete. [AI Ustad's documented capabilities](https://pakeducate.com/features/ai-ustad/)

Keep two checks separate. A fee record needs correct amounts and dates. A draft reminder needs correct facts, an appropriate tone and the right recipient. A fluent message does not prove that the underlying balance is correct. Equally, a correct balance does not make every proposed message suitable to send.

Ask to see how the assistant obtains the information used in a response. Do not assume that a writing assistant automatically edits records or sends messages. For a wider explanation of these distinctions, see our guide to [AI models, assistants and agents](/guides/ai-basics-models-assistants-agents/).

**A fictional fee exercise with an answer key**

The following records are invented for this article. They do not describe a real school or a PakEducate test. They provide a small, inspectable task to bring into a guided demo. Ask the demonstrator how to represent concessions and payments in the supported workflow.

Assume a single monthly fee period, no opening arrears, no penalties and no refunds. An approved concession reduces the amount owed. Payments are amounts already received, not promises to pay.

| Practice student | Monthly charge | Approved concession | Payment received | Expected balance |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Student A | PKR 5,000 | PKR 500 | PKR 3,000 | PKR 1,500 |
| Student B | PKR 5,000 | PKR 0 | PKR 5,000 | PKR 0 |
| Student C | PKR 5,000 | PKR 1,000 | PKR 0 | PKR 4,000 |
| **Total** | **PKR 15,000** | **PKR 1,500** | **PKR 8,000** | **PKR 5,500** |

The check is straightforward: charges minus concessions minus payments equals outstanding balance. Across the three records, PKR 15,000 − PKR 1,500 − PKR 8,000 = PKR 5,500. Student A owes PKR 1,500 for this period, not the original PKR 5,000 charge.

Now imagine a staff member needs an English reminder for Student A's parent. Supply only the approved facts, using placeholders for identifying details:

> Draft a polite fee reminder using these fictional practice facts: monthly charge PKR 5,000; approved concession PKR 500; payment already received PKR 3,000; remaining balance PKR 1,500. Invite the parent to contact the office if their records differ. Do not invent a deadline, penalty, payment link or school policy. Return a draft for staff review; do not send it.

Here is a **deliberately flawed, authored example**, not an observed AI response:

> Your fee of PKR 5,000 is overdue. Pay by tomorrow to avoid a late fine.

It ignores the concession and payment, invents a deadline and adds an unsupported penalty. Even if an AI produced a grammatically perfect version, it would fail the task.

A suitable **authored reference draft** is:

> Dear parent or guardian, our practice record shows a remaining balance of PKR 1,500 for this month's fee after the approved concession and payment received. Please contact the school office if your payment record differs. Thank you.

Before using a similar message in a real workflow, staff must confirm the fee period, current balance, recipient and school-approved wording. For Urdu, ask a fluent staff member to review meaning and tone as well as every number. Translation should preserve the facts without adding promises or consequences.

Try a second step: record an additional fictional PKR 1,000 payment for Student A. The expected balance becomes PKR 500, and the combined outstanding balance becomes PKR 4,500. Generate or edit the reminder again. It should use the new balance; a previously correct message may now be stale.

**What to check during the trial**

Use a few fictional records first. The goal is to observe a complete workflow and record any unresolved questions, rather than judging the system from the appearance of its dashboard.

| Check | Evidence to request or observe |
| --- | --- |
| Staff usability | A teacher completes an attendance task in their preferred language and explains how to correct it. |
| Record accuracy | The fictional balances above match, including after the extra payment. |
| Access | Separate demonstration accounts show what an owner, teacher and parent can see. Ask how access is removed. |
| Parent communication | Staff inspect the recipient and final text before any message is sent. Ask which steps are manual. |
| Reports and exports | Request a sample export, inspect its columns and ask what can be retained if the school leaves. |
| AI assistance | Compare a draft with supplied facts, flag invented details and check the revised version. |
| Support and devices | Try the actual devices and connection staff will use; ask who helps with setup and correction. |
