## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are these free AI tools actually good?
Yes. The free tiers of these tools in 2026 are often more powerful than the most expensive enterprise tools available just two years ago.

### Do I need to enter a credit card?
No, every tool on this list offers a genuine free tier that does not require a credit card to sign up and start using immediately.

### Which tool should I use for coding?
If you are generating UI components from scratch, use v0. If you are building a full application and managing a codebase, download Cursor.

### Are my conversations kept private?
It depends on the tool. For maximum privacy, use Ollama, which runs locally on your machine. For cloud tools like ChatGPT or Claude, you must explicitly opt out of data training in their settings if you want to keep your data private.

### Will these tools remain free?
While pricing models change, major AI companies use free tiers as loss-leaders to acquire users. It is highly likely that robust free AI tools will continue to exist for the foreseeable future.
