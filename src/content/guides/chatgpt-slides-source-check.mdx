**ChatGPT Slides can make an editable presentation from a conversation, outline or source files, but the deck still needs a source check.** OpenAI announced collaborative Slides on **October 16, 2026**. Eligible Pro, Business and Enterprise users with Space access can create and edit a deck in Work or Space; rollout is gradual. On the web, OpenAI lists export to PowerPoint, PDF and Google Slides. [OpenAI release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes) [Slides help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001605-creating-and-collaborating-on-slides-in-chatgpt)

OpenAI says you can change text, shapes, tables and charts directly, request edits to a selected element, share a deck with editors and leave comments. Those features make correction possible; they do not verify a figure or claim on your behalf. This article uses a **fictional source brief and authored answer key**, not a deck AIViewer produced in ChatGPT.

## The source notes: a fictional project update

A community learning group needs a six-slide update for a funder. These short notes are the *entire source* for the exercise:

> - **Quarter 1:** Eight workshops; 96 attendances in total.
> - **Quarter 2:** Ten workshops; 110 attendances in total.
> - **Budget:** $1,800 approved; $1,650 spent by the end of Quarter 2.
> - **Not measured:** Unique people, learning outcomes, satisfaction or the reason attendance changed.

“Attendances” counts visits. A person attending twice may be counted twice. The notes do not justify a claim about unique people reached.

The two useful calculations are **96 ÷ 8 = 12 attendances per workshop in Q1** and **110 ÷ 10 = 11 in Q2**. The total rose by 14 attendances, while the per-workshop average fell by one. The unspent budget is **$1,800 − $1,650 = $150**. None of these numbers explains *why* the change occurred.

## A six-slide plan with an evidence trail

This is an **authored reference plan**. It is the result to request and check, not a screenshot or observed ChatGPT deck.

| Slide | Intended message | Source or calculation | Review limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Scope | Two quarters of community workshops | Source notes | Call them attendances, not unique attendees. |
| 2. Activity | Workshops rose from 8 to 10 | Q1 and Q2 workshop counts | Use the same scale for both bars. |
| 3. Total attendance | Attendances rose from 96 to 110 | Q1 and Q2 attendance totals | Label totals as visits/attendances. |
| 4. Per-session view | Average moved from 12 to 11 | 96/8 and 110/10 | Do not say every session had exactly 12 or 11 people. |
| 5. Budget | $1,650 spent from $1,800 approved; $150 unspent | Budget notes and subtraction | “Unspent” is not a cost saving claim. |
| 6. Next questions | Check unique people and outcomes before claiming reach or impact | “Not measured” note | Present as a proposed measurement step. |

A **deliberately flawed authored caption** for slide 3 would be: “Engagement improved 15% in Q2.” The total attendance increase is 14 ÷ 96 = **14.58%**, or about **14.6%**, but “engagement” was not measured. More workshops could produce more total visits even as the average per workshop falls. A faithful caption is: “Total attendances rose from 96 to 110 across two additional workshops; the average per workshop fell from 12 to 11.”

## Request the deck, then request corrections

For an eligible account, OpenAI's help page says to ask ChatGPT in **Work** to “create ChatGPT slides” from the notes, or use **Create → Slide** in Space. Tell it the audience, length and required source material. A bounded request for this exercise is:

> Create six editable ChatGPT Slides for a funder update from the fictional notes below. Use the six-slide plan in this article. Put the source figure or formula in a visible small source line for each chart. Keep total attendances separate from average attendances per workshop. Do not claim unique reach, outcomes or a cause for the change. Leave the deck for review; do not share or export it yet.

If a slide says “engagement improved,” select the caption and request a precise change: “Replace this with the verified total and average comparison. Remove any claim about engagement or unique people.” OpenAI documents direct editing and element-level edit requests; whether the actual deck follows them accurately still has to be checked. [Slides editing guidance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001605-creating-and-collaborating-on-slides-in-chatgpt)

## Check the file people will receive

Read all six slides at presentation size and scan the tables/charts after each edit. Verify that each number and unit matches the notes or the displayed arithmetic. Then inspect the **exported** PowerPoint, PDF or Google Slides file, because formatting and labels can change in a handoff. OpenAI specifically advises reviewing content and formatting after export. [Slides export guidance](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001605-creating-and-collaborating-on-slides-in-chatgpt)

Try one final exercise: suppose Q2 had **120** attendances instead of 110, with ten workshops. What changes? **Answer:** Q2 average becomes 12 per workshop, matching Q1's 12; total attendances rise by 24, which is 25% of Q1's 96. The budget figures do not change. A deck that updates only the total chart but leaves “average fell” on slide 4 would now be inconsistent.

If Slides is absent, check plan, Space access and rollout before writing a workaround as though it were the same product. OpenAI notes a Windows desktop distinction: some local environments create a PowerPoint file instead of a collaborative Slides deck; web or cloud mode can provide the collaborative route.
