## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which model is better for writing code?
For starting a new project from scratch (green-field development), GPT-5.4's speed and computer-use integration give it the edge. However, for analyzing and refactoring massive legacy codebases, Claude Opus 4.6 is significantly better at holding thousands of lines of context without forgetting parameters.

### Do I get access to these models for free?
No. While both OpenAI and Anthropic offer free tiers (Powered by GPT-5.3 Instant and Claude Haiku 4.5), the flagship Opus 4.6 and GPT-5.4 models are strictly locked behind their respective $20/month Plus and Pro subscriptions.

### Which model is better for creative writing?
Claude Opus 4.6 is universally preferred for creative writing, drafting marketing copy, and writing essays. It possesses a much more natural, varied vocabulary and avoids the repetitive formatting crutches ("In conclusion," "A tapestry of") that GPT-5.4 still defaults to.

### Does GPT-5.4 hallucinate less than Claude?
Both models hallucinate far less than their 2024 predecessors. However, inside massive 100k+ token documents, GPT-5.4 is slightly more prone to hallucinating connections that don't exist, whereas Claude Opus 4.6 boasts near-perfect factual recall within its context window.

### Should I subscribe to ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro?
If you are doing data analysis, coding from scratch, or need autonomous agents, buy ChatGPT Plus. If you are doing deep research on massive PDFs, writing long-form content, or refactoring legacy codebases, buy Claude Pro.
