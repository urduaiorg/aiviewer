Consistency is the hardest part of content creation. This prompt uses [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/) to eliminate the blank page and instantly generate a structured, 30-day content calendar tailored to your specific niche and target audience.

## The Prompt

<PromptBlock 
  tool="ChatGPT"
  prompt={`Act as an expert social media strategist and content planner. I need a 30-day content calendar for my [PLATFORM, e.g., Instagram/LinkedIn] account. 

My niche is: [YOUR NICHE, e.g., personal finance for millennials].
My target audience is: [YOUR AUDIENCE].
My primary goal is: [GOAL, e.g., grow followers and drive newsletter signups].

Please create a markdown table with 30 rows (one for each day) containing the following columns:
1. Day (1-30)
2. Content Pillar (e.g., Educational, Entertaining, Personal Story, Promotional)
3. Hook / Headline (The first sentence to grab attention)
4. Post Concept (A brief 1-sentence description of the visual or video idea)
5. Call to Action (What the user should do next)

Ensure the content mix is balanced (not too promotional) and follows current best practices for engagement on this platform.`}
/>

## Example Output

When you run this prompt, ChatGPT will output a clean, actionable table:

> Here is your 30-day Instagram content calendar focusing on personal finance for millennials:
> 
> | Day | Content Pillar | Hook / Headline | Post Concept | Call to Action |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | Educational | "Stop making this $10,000 mistake with your 401(k)." | Carousel showing the difference between pre-tax and Roth contributions. | Save this post for your next HR meeting! |
> | 2 | Personal Story | "I had $30k in student debt at 25. Here is exactly how I paid it off." | Talking-head Reel walking through my specific budget breakdown. | Drop a 💸 in the comments if you're debt-free tracking. |
> | 3 | Entertaining | "When payday hits but the rent is due the next morning." | Relatable meme/GIF with a trending audio track. | Share this to your Story if you feel the pain. |

## Tips for Customization

* **Specify your pillars:** If you already know your brand pillars (e.g., "Behind the Scenes," "Client Wins," "How-to"), list them explicitly in the prompt so the AI sticks to them.
* **Request script generation:** Once you see a concept you like in the table, you can reply: "Please write the full video script for Day 2."
* **Platform constraints:** Mention any platform limits, such as "Keep all Twitter hooks under 280 characters" or "Focus entirely on short-form vertical video concepts."
