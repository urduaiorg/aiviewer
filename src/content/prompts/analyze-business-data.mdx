Small business owners often have access to data (like sales spreadsheets or customer feedback logs) but lack the time to analyze it. This prompt leverages [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/) (specifically GPT-5.4's Advanced Data Analysis) to digest raw data and output clear, actionable business recommendations.

## The Prompt

<PromptBlock 
  tool="ChatGPT"
  prompt={`Act as an expert business analyst and fractional CFO. I am going to provide you with raw data representing my small business's [TYPE OF DATA, e.g., monthly sales for 2025]. 

Please analyze this data and provide the following:
1. Executive Summary: A 3-sentence overview of the primary trend.
2. Key Anomalies: Identify any surprising spikes, drops, or patterns that stand out.
3. Profitability Opportunities: Suggest 3 specific, actionable ways I can cut costs or increase revenue based on this data.
4. Next Steps: Recommend the top 2 immediate actions I should take this week.

Here is the data:
[PASTE DATA OR ATTACH CSV HERE]`}
/>

## Example Output

When you run this prompt with a pasted sales report, ChatGPT will generate a concise executive brief:

> **Executive Summary:**
> Your business experienced a steady 15% revenue growth throughout Q1 and Q2, but saw a sharp 22% drop in Q3, specifically in the "Consulting Services" tier. Overall, your software subscription revenue remained stable, acting as a reliable baseline during the Q3 dip.
> 
> **Key Anomalies:**
> - **August Slump:** August sales were 40% below the yearly average. This correlates with the drop in consulting hours billed.
> - **High-Margin Winner:** Your "Premium Support" package accounts for only 10% of total sales volume but 35% of total profit margin.

## Tips for Customization

* **Sanitize your data:** Before pasting, remove any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) like customer names or exact credit card details.
* **Provide context:** Let the AI know what industry you're in (e.g., "I run a local coffee shop") so its recommendations are tailored to your physical reality.
* **Ask for charts:** If you are using ChatGPT Plus, you can add "Please generate a bar chart visualizing this trend" to get presentation-ready graphics.
