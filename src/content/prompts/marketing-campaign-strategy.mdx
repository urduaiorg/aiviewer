## How Should You Customize This Prompt?

- **Change the Product:** Replace "eco-friendly water bottle" with your actual product.
- **Change the Audience:** Replace "college students" with your target demographic (e.g., "busy professionals", "new parents").
- **Adjust the Timeline:** Change "4-week" to whatever timeline fits your launch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which AI model is best for this prompt?
Claude Opus 4.6 is highly recommended for this prompt due to its ability to handle complex formatting and produce nuanced marketing copy.

### How long should the final output be?
Depending on the model, you should expect a detailed 3-5 page strategy document that breaks down each channel and timeline phase.

### Can the AI generate the actual ad creatives too?
This prompt focuses on strategy, but you can follow up by asking the AI to write the specific copy for the Facebook ads or email sequences it suggested.

### What if the AI suggests an unrealistic budget allocation?
You should treat the AI's output as a first draft and manually adjust the budget allocations based on your actual historical customer acquisition costs.
