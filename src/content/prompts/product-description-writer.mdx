Writing hundreds of product descriptions is one of the most tedious parts of running an e-commerce store. However, using GPT-5.4, you can instantly turn a bulleted list of dry technical specs into persuasive, SEO-optimized sales copy that converts.

This prompt is designed to focus on the **benefits** of your product rather than just the features, answering the customer's implicit question: *"What's in it for me?"*

## The Prompt

<PromptBlock 
  tool="ChatGPT (GPT-5.4)" 
  prompt={`You are an elite e-commerce copywriter specializing in high-converting product descriptions for [Shopify/Amazon]. 

I am going to provide you with the name of a product, its target audience, and a list of technical features. 

Your task is to write a 150-200 word product description that:
1. Opens with a strong, emotion-driven hook targeting the audience's core pain point.
2. Translates at least 3 technical features into clear, lifestyle benefits (use bullet points).
3. Includes semantic SEO keywords naturally.
4. Ends with a clear, urgent call to action (CTA).

Product Name: [Insert Name]
Target Audience: [Insert Audience]
Technical Features: [Insert Comma-separated Specs]`}
/>

## Example Output

**Product:** "Lumaflex Core Posture Corrector"
**Target Audience:** Office workers with back pain
**Output:**

*Tired of ending your workday with a stiff neck and aching lower back? The Lumaflex Core Posture Corrector isn't just a brace—it's your invisible daily posture coach, designed specifically for desk-bound professionals.*

*Instead of temporary relief, Lumaflex guarantees long-term spine health by gently realigning your shoulders without restricting your movement.*

* **All-Day Comfort:** Made with breathable, aerospace-grade neoprene so you stay cool and sweat-free through back-to-back meetings.
* **Invisible Under Clothes:** Our ultra-slim profile means you can wear it discreetly under your dress shirt or blouse. No bulky outlines.
* **Memory-Foam Spine Support:** Automatically adapts to the unique curvature of your back, providing targeted relief exactly where tension builds up.*

*Stop letting back pain dictate your productivity. Reclaim your focus and sit taller today. [Add to Cart] to experience posture correction that actually feels good.*

## Tips for Best Results

* **Specify the Platform:** Mentioning Amazon will cause the AI to format the output with keyword-stuffed bullet points (ideal for Amazon algorithms), whereas specifying Shopify will yield a more narrative, brand-focused tone.
* **Feed It Customer Reviews:** If you already have existing reviews, paste them below the features. GPT-5.4 is excellent at incorporating exact phrases your happy customers use.
