Writing dozens or hundreds of product descriptions is one of the most tedious tasks for an e-commerce business. This prompt uses [ChatGPT](/tools/chatgpt/) to instantly convert basic product specs into persuasive, SEO-friendly copy that drives conversions.

## The Prompt

<PromptBlock 
  tool="ChatGPT"
  prompt={`Act as an expert e-commerce copywriter and SEO specialist. I need a high-converting product description for a new item on my Shopify store. 

Product Name: [INSERT PRODUCT NAME]
Target Audience: [INSERT TARGET AUDIENCE]
Key Features/Specs: 
- [SPEC 1]
- [SPEC 2]
- [SPEC 3]

Please write a product description that includes:
1. An attention-grabbing hook (1-2 sentences) that speaks directly to the customer's pain point or desire.
2. A descriptive paragraph highlighting the primary benefit of the product (focus on benefits, not just features).
3. A scannable bulleted list of 4-5 key features.
4. A strong Call to Action (CTA) to drive the purchase.

Keep the tone [INSERT TONE, e.g., playful, luxurious, minimalist, urgent]. Ensure the primary keyword "[INSERT KEYWORD]" is naturally used at least twice for SEO.`}
/>

## Example Output

When you run this prompt, ChatGPT will deliver ready-to-publish store copy:

> **Hook:** Tired of replacing your charging cables every three months? Meet the last cable you will ever need to buy.
> 
> **Description:** The TitanBraid USB-C Cable is engineered for those who demand durability without sacrificing charging speed. Wrapped in military-grade ballistic nylon, this heavy-duty charging cable resists fraying, pet chewing, and the daily wear-and-tear of your commute. Whether you're tossing it into a backpack or stretching it across the couch, you get a reliable, high-speed charge every single time.
> 
> **Key Features:**
> - **Military-Grade Braiding:** Tested to withstand 20,000+ bends.
> - **Hyper-Fast Charging:** Supports up to 100W power delivery.
> - **Tangle-Free Design:** Naturally coils without knotting.
> 
> **CTA:** Stop wasting money on fragile cords. Upgrade your setup and add the TitanBraid to your cart today!

## Tips for Customization

* **Batch process:** If you have 10 similar products, you can ask ChatGPT to output the descriptions for all 10 in a CSV format so you can bulk-import them into Shopify.
* **Match your brand voice:** If your brand is highly sarcastic or highly clinical, give the AI an example of your past writing and say "Mimic this exact tone of voice."
* **Add constraints:** If your website design has a strict character limit for product pages, add "Keep the total output under 150 words" to the prompt.
