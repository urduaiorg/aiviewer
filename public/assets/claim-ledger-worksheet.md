## Fictional worked example

Source: 40 registered; 30 attended; 20 attendees answered a usefulness survey; 18 said useful; no follow-up outcome data.

Flawed summary: The workshop reached 40 people, achieved 90% satisfaction among participants, and improved their employment prospects.

| Claim | Check | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Reached 40 | Registration differs from attendance | State 30 attended out of 40 registered (75%) |
| 90% satisfaction among participants | 18/20 respondents said useful | State 90% of respondents described it as useful |
| Improved employment prospects | No follow-up data | Remove the unsupported outcome claim |

Corrected: Thirty of 40 registered participants attended (75%). Eighteen of 20 survey respondents described the workshop as useful. No follow-up outcome data has been collected.

These are authored teaching examples, not real participant results or observed model output.
