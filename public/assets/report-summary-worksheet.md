## Before drafting

- Identify the source pages, tables, and notes available to you.
- State which details the reader needs to make the intended decision.
- Identify gaps, unreadable sections, and information that is out of scope.

## Prompt to adapt

Summarize the supplied report for [reader and purpose], using only that report. Preserve the main results, important figures and their denominators, limitations, and decision status. Do not invent benefits, approvals, owners, or dates. After the summary, list each numerical or decision-related claim with its supporting page or section. Mark missing information as unknown. Use a length appropriate to the task.

- Recalculate important numbers with the correct denominators.
- Check that a request, proposal, or recommendation did not become an approval.
- Preserve exclusions and uncertainty that could affect the decision.
- Confirm that the summary answers its reader's actual question.
